## Supply-record semantics

- Persist a trusted registry product ID and a positive integer **purchase quantity**, with user notes/purpose. Derive product status, offers, pack contents and qualifications from the registry. Never accept caller-authored `verified`, role, stock or suitability flags.
- A supply reference needs a separate identity/pack check from `checkProductBinding`: the existing binding check correctly rejects unknown geometry. Adding a supply must not bypass that check for placed parts or grant a mesh/support/collision role.
- Render/export “1 socket pair” or “1 top/bottom tape kit,” with contained quantities. `profile.packCount` alone cannot describe heterogeneous contents. A typed composition of roles is preferable to a generic units-per-pack division.
- If later computing from demand, one open plus one closed socket per pack means `max(openNeeded,closedNeeded)` packs. Tape kit count would be `max(ceil(topFeet/32),ceil(bottomFeet/32))`, after the user has supplied source-appropriate footage/allowances. These arithmetic examples do not determine the project’s required sockets, tape runs or installation scheme.
- Hinge/stay purchase counts may be explicitly user-selected while the product-specific fit question remains open. Do not mark a generated lid mechanism complete merely because a hinge and support appear in a list.
- Keep supply procurement separate from placed hardware and recorded joints, or explicitly link them by physical identity. Until linked, show possible duplicate representation; do not automatically subtract or silently double-count.
- Use the configured retailer/store/ZIP snapshot for price and availability of the exact supply pack. A product reference is not a snapshot. A supplier ID outside today’s editable `MaterialId` enum will need its own bounded snapshot/reference association rather than a fabricated geometry material.

The included parts of a Henley kit follow the same containment principle at a larger scale: count the whole kit once and its included children zero additional purchases. Standalone accessory selections must not also charge for components already contained by a selected kit.
