The treated post’s actual fields resolve a 35 in decimal-drop highlight; specific treatment retention/use category remain unverified. Its typed `placementOnly` restriction excludes all automatic structure choices and rejects direct selection before registry lookup, including an unknown registry reference. Manual placement, cuts, cut-source/BOM counting and exact snapshots remain supported. Neither product receives sauna role qualifications.

Plytanium’s [manufacturer table](https://www.buildgp.com/product/plytanium-plywood-sanded-panels) supports the 0.703 in thickness category and casework applications. The role qualification is restricted to dry interior casework because exposure descriptions conflict. Measure the purchased panel before precision joinery. It must not replace existing 0.75 in geometry silently.

Panel and rod geometry are **external envelopes**. SUNLITE is multiwall rather than solid polycarbonate; closet tube wall thickness/inside diameter are unknown. Neither envelope is evidence of material strength. Screw/nail display envelopes use head diameter; head width must never be substituted for shank diameter in penetration or hole-fit reasoning.

Shopping quantities round required physical stock into purchase packs once. Linear board bins, packed sheets and validated physical cut sources are counted before pack conversion. `physicalStockUnits` records that requirement; `quantity` remains the sell-unit count, with explicit `packCount`, purchased-stock and surplus counts. For example, seven required boards in a six-board bundle would buy two bundles and leave five surplus boards; the cut layout still contains seven physical sources. No new six-board material is seeded by this arithmetic change.

Hardware uses the same one-time conversion: 101 SD9112R100 screws require two100-count boxes;121 N10DHDG-R nails require two120-count boxes. Added visible hardware pieces and joint hardware both contribute; duplicate representation of the same physical hardware must be reviewed. Tools never enter this count.

## Snapshot inventory and BOM limits

The shipped blank project has `inventory: []`. The inventory format example has a **2020** timestamp, placeholder identity/store and zero availability; it is not a store observation. A usable snapshot must match the selected retailer, material, store and ZIP, be no older than 24 hours, and not be in the future. For a registered exact offer, the snapshot product number/URL must identify that exact variant. Other manually matched stock remains user-observed evidence rather than a curated cross-retailer equivalence.

Quantity and price mean the material’s **purchase unit**: boards, sheets, panels, rods, hardware boxes or explicitly defined bundles. Snapshots must report quantities and prices for those exact sell units, not individual screws inside a box or a wholesale carton that differs from the reviewed pack. Physical-stock and surplus counts are separate. Store selection remains configurable with default ZIP 84121; a default ZIP alone does not identify a store.

The 21 generic choices still support dimensional planning and example estimates; they are not 21 verified retailer products. Known cut-stock lineage counts one original source once. New cuttable sheets use a feasible layout with kerf rather than area alone; this does not claim globally optimal yield. Invalid lineage, oversized parts, unresolved hardware or unpriced products can leave the shopping list incomplete. An exact material/product binding does not resolve missing sockets, greenhouse edge/roof details, lid stays or sauna-specific materials.
