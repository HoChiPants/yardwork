# Retailer catalog research and an initial curated basis

Researched **November 16, 2026**, using public retailer and manufacturer pages. This is a proposed data foundation, not a claim that the running app mirrors either retailer's complete catalog. No account was connected, no catalog was bulk-downloaded, and no store inventory was checked for ZIP 84121. Product identity evidence and local availability evidence are separate.

## What the current implementation can represent

At inspection, `src/model.ts` has 21 generic material families with actual modeled dimensions, stock kind, cuttability and pack count. Pieces reference a family such as `stud` or `connector-screw`; they do not select an immutable manufacturer/retailer variant. `inventorySchema` requires a Home Depot HTTPS URL, an `internetNumber`, a generic material ID, numeric price/quantity, store, ZIP and timestamp. `InventoryForm.tsx` relies on a human confirming a physical stock match. The optional server adapter validates that same schema and exact store/ZIP.

Consequently, simply allowing a Lowe's URL would not establish equivalent products. The schema currently cannot preserve manufacturer identity, retailer-specific item IDs, seller, variant-specific dimensions, uncertain/unknown stock, fulfillment channel, conflicting specifications, or reusable tools. Its sample 3/4-inch panel, 0.18-inch connector screw and 2 × 2 × 1.5-inch bracket must not silently become the differently sized real products below.

## Identity, geometry and evidence contract

Use three separate records rather than expanding a generic material string into a purported retailer mirror:

1. **Physical/pack variant:** stable internal ID, manufacturer, complete manufacturer part number, verified GTIN when available, product family, nominal description, actual dimensions and their meanings, tolerance, grade/species/treatment/finish, individual-unit geometry, pack count and sell unit. A geometry family may share a body across 100/500 packs; each purchasable variant keeps its own complete part number/pack identity.
2. **Retail offer:** retailer (`home-depot` or `lowes`), country, retailer product ID, store SKU/item number separately, seller, exact product URL, physical variant ID, lifecycle status and verification source/date. Keep URL IDs as strings; a Lowe's URL product ID is not its displayed item number or a Home Depot Internet number.
3. **Observation:** offer ID, retailer/store/ZIP, fulfillment channel, `availableUnits: number | null`, availability status, price amount/currency or null, observed/expiry times, evidence method (`user-snapshot`, approved API/feed), provenance, and any source timestamp. Unknown quantity remains unknown. Shipping, delivery and pickup are separate observations.

Each meaningful field needs its own source URL, retrieval/review date and confidence/status. Use states such as `manufacturer-spec`, `retailer-spec`, `measured`, `approximate-rendering`, `conflict`, `retired`, and `unreviewed`. A source row's timestamp is not an inventory-check timestamp. Preserve conflicts instead of silently preferring whichever value is larger.

Selecting a real variant should create a reviewed binding to actual modeled stock; it must not resize already-cut parts, change a fastener schedule or clear an error invisibly. Preview the changed fit/cut/quantity effects. Count hardware once in physical units, then round up by the chosen offer's pack count. Keep tools outside the consumable BOM; track owned/borrowed/to-buy separately.

## Availability and implementation acceptance

The public SD screw page returned shipping/delivery counts associated with a different named store while pickup was unavailable. This is evidence of why a fetched product page is **not a ZIP 84121 stock snapshot**. No quantity or price from this research belongs in seeded inventory.

An initial registry should contain only reviewed identity/specification records and exact product links. Start all availability unknown. Continue user snapshots until approved data access is configured. Test that wrong retailer/store/ZIP, stale/future timestamps, retired listings, third-party sellers, different lengths/finishes and mismatched pack sizes cannot produce a green “all materials available” claim. Test 101 SD screws against the 100-count variant as two packs; test the same quantity against a separately selected 500-count variant as one pack; do not merge the offers.

For the greenhouse, sauna, banquette, shelves and closet digital builds, record the selected physical variants and unmatched requirements separately. A complete quantity list with unverified material suitability, missing glazing/heater/hinges/anchors, or unknown stock remains incomplete. A generic stud/sheet proxy can support layout studies but must remain visibly unbound to a purchasable variant.

## Data/media permissions and unresolved access

Publicly readable facts, images, CAD and marketing copy do not share one blanket permission. The [Simpson drawing page](https://www.strongtie.com/framinganglesandplates_anglesandplates/a_angle/p/a) points to download terms and describes drawings as approximate product geometry. The [HD affiliate FAQ](https://www.homedepot.com/c/SF_MS_Affiliate_Program_FAQs) says program policies are supplied through its application/acceptance process. The actual partner/feed/CAD licenses, caching limits, trademark/media permissions and redistribution rights for this project have **not been established**. This is an implementation/access finding, not legal advice.

Keep the app's code license separate from third-party assets. For now, retain hand-reviewed facts and links, use original procedural visuals labeled with their geometry precision, and do not ship retailer photographs or downloaded manufacturer CAD as MIT-licensed assets. No bypass of retailer protections, internal storefront endpoint reuse, or credential harvesting was attempted.
