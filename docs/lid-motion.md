# Lid travel and retained-solid overlap

Select a storage bench in **Sections**, then choose **Review lid travel**. **Play opening** and **Play closing** stop before a detected obstruction and highlight the parts involved. Use the slider, the 0° and 75° inspection buttons, or a named finding to examine either side of an obstruction. The camera frames the full swing. You can orbit freely and use **Fit project** to restore that framing. The blue line marks the hinge axis.

This is a temporary, read-only view. Exiting, changing section/tab or editing the project restores its saved pose. Saving or exporting while scrubbing saves the actual project, without the temporary angle. The separate **Preview lid open** checkbox retains the existing saved open/closed behavior. Cuts, source-stock lineage, names, IDs, quantities, notes and history survive the rigid lid movement. A generated `seat-lid` role identifies a renamed lid; legacy name matching must be unique.

The solver checks the retained part geometry along the swing and subdivides close passes. It reports confirmed interference, possible contact with exterior envelopes, and unresolved geometry or close passes. The work zone supplies room boundaries. Measured wall and baseboard faces include the requested minimum gap. The model treats contacts within 0.01 inches as touching for part-to-part checks. Use actual hardware and field measurements to determine fabrication tolerances.

Project checks now also compare rotated wood and saved convex cut profiles. They use the retained shape rather than only its enclosing box; resting face contact and removed cut-away stock are excluded. Corrugated sheet and multiwall glazing overlaps are reported separately as envelope reviews. Tubes and hardware are not silently turned into filled rectangular solids. The convex overlap method follows [David Eberly’s separating-axis treatment](https://www.geometrictools.com/Documentation/MethodOfSeparatingAxes.pdf).

The opening uses the selected measured hinge axis when available and otherwise uses a provisional rear-edge pivot. Verify hinge geometry and mounting, lid supports, pinch points, and seat loads on the actual assembly. For custom doors, lids, and drawers, use **Test movement** to specify a hinge or slide and hold nearby mechanisms at different openings.

For a door or drawer in a rotated cabinet, use **Moving part axes**. Set the leaf or drawer box as the reference and include its handle and other moving parts in the same group. Preset hinge edges follow the reference part. **Workspace axes** and exact workspace pivot coordinates are available for hardware located from a room datum.

For a rigid hinge against fixed trim, **Test movement** checks the rotating part across the angle interval. Play pauses before contact or an interval that needs review. Open and closed inspection are still available. In the practice project, the two endpoints clear the trim and playback stops at different angles from each side. [Door and trim practice plan](../public/examples/motion/door-trim-swing.yardwork.json) · [Build guide](../public/examples/motion/door-trim-swing-guide.md) · [Blum angle-stop example](https://ea.blum.com/en/opening-angle-stops-155-hinges/)

Playback and inspection use temporary poses. Saved projects retain their explicit open or closed design pose.

In **Test movement**, choose **Save test setup** to keep nearby held positions or a linked motion with the selected movement. Save the project to reopen the setup from the library. The printed and Markdown guides list the saved arrangement and the clearance findings for the current model. Held positions are percentages of each motion’s travel, so changing a motion’s range changes where it is held.

In **Check a minimum gap**, choose two modeled parts and run the check. **Find closest gap** measures the same pair through the test, including both legs when movements run in order. The result gives a gap at a specific pose and a range for the modeled minimum. A wider range means the search reached its limit while there was still uncertainty between poses. Use **Inspect closest pose** to highlight the two parts at the reported position. When a gap is present, a dimension line connects the two closest points. The position is a temporary preview. Use the hardware instructions and installed dimensions to choose the required gap. [Blum minimum hinge gap tables](https://publications.blum.com/2026/catalogue/en/71/) · [Accuride drawer slide mounting dimensions](https://www.accuride.com/media/amasty/amfile/attach/a3bcba75b0b82de060a661de95ce9f44.pdf)

When the active test specifies a minimum gap, **Play opening** and **Play closing** stop before the first part of the path where Yardwork cannot confirm that gap. The canvas identifies the minimum-gap stop. Use the slider or endpoint buttons to inspect past it. A named test keeps its minimum gap when you reopen the project.

Import the drawer practice plan and select **Drawer lip check**. Both endpoints clear the lip. The modeled gap narrows to 1/16 in midway, so the saved 1/8 in test pauses near 4.39 in opening and 7.61 in closing. The guide gives the cuts for both panels from one measured 8 × 6 in plywood scrap and lists the three remnants. To measure a pair from **Plan an idea**, ask **“What's the closest gap between P009 and P003?”**. Choose the saved motion or named test and select **Measure this gap**. You can also give the test's exact name in the question. The ruler uses the saved travel sequence and held positions in that test. After **Edit obstruction**, **Retest clearance** measures the same pair in your updated layout. A measurement request uses the modeled geometry directly and leaves any required minimum to the saved test or your entry.

When a hinged door and sliding drawer are linked, open **Find a door opening for the drawer** and enter the extension you need. You can also ask **Plan an idea** how far to open the door before pulling out the drawer. The search checks whole-degree openings and the reachable endpoint with the drawer closed first. It then checks drawer extension, the reverse closing path, held parts, site clearances and any requested minimum gap. An opening that works can be applied to the working test, saved under a name and exported with the build guide. The result is the first passing tested angle. Inspect the actual hinge, mounting plate, door thickness and stop to verify the cabinet setup. [Blum hinge applications](https://publications.blum.com/2024/catalogue/en/74/)

If the hardware has a fixed stop, enter its measured or published angle under **Find a door opening for the drawer** and choose **Check door stop**. You can also ask **“Will an 86.25 degree door stop let my drawer open 12.5 inches?”** Yardwork tests that exact angle, including the door swing with the drawer retracted and the reverse closing path. Use the actual hinge path and installed stop when measuring. [Blum opening-angle stop instructions](https://www.blum.com/us/en/products/hingesystems/clip-top-blumotion/downloads-videos/)

If the door clears and the drawer cannot reach the requested extension, choose **Find shorter drawer travel**. Yardwork searches in ⅛ in increments with the same door stop, held parts and selected gap. **Use shorter sequence** loads the verified opening and closing sequence so you can save its measured targets in the build guide. The saved slide travel stays available for other tests. [Accuride slide travel and side-space guidance](https://www.accuride.com/media/amasty/amfile/attach/a3bcba75b0b82de060a661de95ce9f44.pdf)

Try this workflow with the [editable cabinet](../public/examples/hinges/fixed-stop-drawer.yardwork.json). It includes a measured aisle edge, three named motion tests, and a plywood mounting spacer with recorded cuts and two reusable remnants.

Use **Compare door stops** to test candidate stop angles with the same requested extension and cabinet layout. The example verifies 5⅜ in of travel at 35° and 6⅝ in at 55°. At 90° the door reaches the measured aisle edge. Ask **“Compare 35°, 55°, and 90° door stops for 12.5 in of drawer travel”**, then choose a row to play its route or inspect the obstruction. Confirm the installed hinge path and the stops offered for the selected hardware. [Blum hinge planning and opening-angle stops](https://publications.blum.com/2026/catalogue/en/79/)

In **Fit a drawer box**, open **Drawer slide spacers** to add fixed layers on either side. The actual plywood thickness determines the mounting faces and the drawer's width and position. The selected slide still needs its specified side space at both mounting faces. Ask **“Plan a drawer between P001 and P002 with two left spacer layers”** to start with a specific arrangement. Preview the cut sizes and select matching scraps for the drawer panels and spacer strips. [Blum guidance on spacing drawers past a door front](https://publications.blum.com/2024/catalogue/en/355/) · [Accuride 3732 mounting dimensions](https://www.accuride.com/media/amasty/amfile/attach/a3bcba75b0b82de060a661de95ce9f44.pdf)

Open **Check this drawer with a cabinet door** before adding the draft. Choose the saved hinge, stop angle and drawer travel. The workspace shows the verified endpoint or the first modeled obstruction. Adding the drawer keeps the checked sequence on its motion so the guide can report it with the cuts. Confirm how the spacer layers and slide fasteners attach to the actual cabinet.

The [cabinet spacer practice plan](../public/examples/hinges/drawer-slide-spacers.yardwork.json) uses two 0.703 in plywood layers on the left. Open **Test movement** on the inset drawer to play its saved 5 in sequence at a 35° door stop. To try the gap search, check the drawer's left side **P009** against the door **P003** with a practice minimum of **0.0625 in**. The closest checked pose is at 5 in of drawer travel, with a modeled gap of about 0.389 in. The build guide records the cuts for both spacers and the two remnants left by the first spacer's cut.

To compare several arrangements, run the test and open **Keep this test under a name**. Give each arrangement a name, such as “Open door first” or “Both drawers open.” Switch between them with **Clearance test**. Enter a different name when you want to keep the original arrangement and save a variation. In **Guide → Review build preparation**, choose a named result to reopen its test at the reported pose. Named tests use the current model each time they run.

Open **Guide → Review build preparation → Motion and clearance** to inspect a flagged test. Each row identifies the affected parts or measured site face and opens the relevant motion at the finding. A missing part or companion opens the setup with its error so you can revise it.

Each built-in storage lid also appears in that checklist when its swing has an obstruction, a measured clearance issue or an incomplete check. A row opens the affected seat at the relevant angle when one is available. Folding stay findings use the entered reach and arm dimensions.

With measured widths and thicknesses, Yardwork checks the two pin-to-pin arm rectangles throughout opening and closing. It uses the linkage's range of angles and pin positions to rule out intersections over each interval. Playback stops before a confirmed overlap or at the first interval that needs review. **Inspect arm clearance** shows uncertain parts and the affected stay in amber. Inspect the actual hardware for bracket, pivot, pinch and locking clearances. ## Check drawer travel with a fitted pull

In **Fit a drawer box**, turn on **Add a centered cabinet pull**. The Liberty P01012-PC-C uses the 96 mm mounting spacing and extends 1.3 in from either the box front or the optional finished front. Its clearance envelope travels with the drawer in the slide-only and combined door tests. The guide keeps it with the assembly hardware and panel cuts, and the Home Depot list counts one pull per placed part. If a fixed face or a cabinet door limits travel, preview the contact pose and adjust the fit before cutting. Check the actual pull, screw reach through any finished front, and room to grip the handle. [Liberty specifications](https://www.libertyhardware.com/products/liberty-solid-bar-pull) · [Home Depot product dimensions](https://www.homedepot.com/p/204331182)

You can also ask **“Plan a drawer between P001 and P002 with a cabinet pull.”** Use the labels of your modeled cabinet sides. Import the [drawer pull clearance practice project](../public/examples/hinges/drawer-bar-pull.yardwork.json) to see the pull approach the opposing cabinet.
