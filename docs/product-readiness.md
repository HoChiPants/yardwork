# Product readiness

Yardwork is a usable local planning prototype. It is not a construction-ready design service or a product ready for a retailer partnership. The overall product goal remains open.

## Working foundation

The editor starts blank and supports configurable work zones, independent measurement units, direct movement/rotation, snapping, exact positioning, measurement, straight and angled cuts in wood, and retained offcuts. Projects save to a local library, reopen and export/import editable JSON. Scaled DXF linework is a reference import, not automatic extraction of a dependable framed structure.

Guides generate from actual parts, cuts and recorded connections. Explicit part-stage choices, saved task order and custom notes survive regeneration. Current software checks cover source accounting, geometry conflicts, stale references and entered fastener centerlines. These checks are not strength calculations or installation approval.

The guide compares saved step order against modeled assembly prerequisites and the actual members named in saved hardware plans. This covers shelf-pin drilling review before shelf placement, screw and bracket setup before joining the planned pair, banquette hinge review between the shell and lid, and furniture restraint review before the final check. Build preparation links to the affected step and names the prerequisite by its current step number. Markdown and print carry the same findings. Custom and unmodeled tasks still rely on the builder's intended sequence.

The catalog has 62 editable stocks. The registry contains 128 product records and 146 retailer references, including historical offers. The Home Depot workflow uses configurable store and ZIP snapshots for observed price and quantity. The default ZIP is 84121.

## Main remaining milestones

1. **Catalog coverage:** source-backed choices for the materials and hardware offered in normal building flows. Generic examples and incomplete product bindings must remain visibly distinct. Broaden common sizes, fasteners, brackets and tools while keeping exact purchase packs and store observations separate.
2. **Construction reasoning:** represent complete joints, bearing/bracing/anchorage and load paths, then provide specific weakness/fix suggestions with their evidence and limits. Nearby faces or a valid screw centerline do not establish a strong joint.
3. **Complete project workflows:** close the build-specific gaps above, improve cutting sequences/offcut reuse and test complete purchasing and assembly handoffs. The editor models width miters, thickness bevels and compound end cuts from corner measurements, with retained shape views for overlapping blank extents. General joinery editing and tool-specific saw settings remain open.
4. **AI and editing:** broaden reliable idea generation and refinement beyond supported recipe/module families. Local presets are disclosed; optional model-driven planning needs broader current fidelity/performance qualification. Focused phone/tablet inspection and one active screw-location caption are implemented; broader responsive layout, direct construction editing and performance still need work.
5. **Commercial operation:** qualify onboarding, recovery, accessibility, performance, authentication/hosting/backups and security for the intended deployment. The local single-process save service and Docker workflow are not a multi-user hosted release. Retailer account integrations, accepted project import and commercial partnerships are not established.
6. **Future trades/code:** add plumbing, sprinklers, electrical, roofing and HVAC through explicit trade models and versioned jurisdiction/rule packs. Dated Utah references, measured through-wall vent and dryer exhaust checks, and residential asphalt-shingle pitch and underlayment comparisons are implemented. Field-shingle estimates follow retained deck surfaces and require field-confirmed bundle coverage. Named eave, rake, hip and ridge runs feed GAF starter and cap bundle estimates. Eave and rake runs also produce a lap-aware drip edge cut list with shared sticks, leftover lengths and a documented cutting tool. Utah review compares entered lap, installed dimensions, fastener spacing and underlayment order. Other flashing, deck protection, fastener selection and local applicability still need review. Full code grading and certified engineering remain future work.

Current implementation/test/container/visual evidence and its limits are recorded in [requirements-audit.md](requirements-audit.md). A green regression suite verifies software behavior within those cases; it does not complete the milestones above.
