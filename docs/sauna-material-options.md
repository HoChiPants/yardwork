# Sauna material options with source evidence

Original research dates to 2026-11-16. Historical proposal data is in [sauna-material-proposals.json](sauna-material-proposals.json). Yardwork's retailer prices and availability come from selected-store snapshots.

There are two useful implementation paths: a custom framed room using individually specified sauna materials from specialist manufacturers, and a complete retailer-sold sauna kit represented as one assembly. Neither requires pretending generic closet cedar is qualified hot-room lining.

Cedarbrook's home sauna guidance describes R-26 or greater at the ceiling and a sauna foil layer. Finnleo's custom-cut kit has a separate assembly specification. Confirm the product, facing, heater, layer sequence and room-system compatibility together. [Cedarbrook insulation guidance](https://cedarbrooksauna.com/what-insulation-should-be-used-in-a-home-sauna/) · [Finnleo custom-cut installation](https://www.finnleo.com/hubfs/Finnleo%20Downloads/710-0101%20Custom%20Cut%20Instruction%2012-18-2018.pdf)

A complete custom-room project still needs the chosen heater/control/sensor/fence package and its clearance/ventilation requirements, compatible attachment hardware, floor/water management, bench framing/support details and any exterior weather layers. This is a concrete product-selection path; it is not a declaration that the room is ready to construct. Existing aromatic closet cedar, pressure-treated lumber and generic interior doors should not gain a sauna qualification from their material names. For example, [EightDoors’ response on its ordinary pine interior door](https://www.homedepot.com/p/questions/eightdoors-36-in-x-80-in-x-1-3-8-in-2-Panel-Solid-Core-Unfinished-Arch-Top-V-Groove-Knotty-Pine-Wood-Interior-Door-Slab-13288005803635/317057979/3) sets temperature/humidity limits incompatible with assuming every wood door can serve this role.

The manufacturer describes a traditional outdoor sauna with solid 30 mm cedar walls and an 8 mm tempered glass door. Its FAQ says no chemicals, stains or glues are used inside, and describes the outdoor walls as solid kiln-dried cedar rather than a synthetic-insulation assembly. Preserve that kit construction: **do not add fictitious foil/batt layers or turn kit walls into separately sold T&G stock**.

Store one complete kit purchase unit. A component list later extracted from its actual manual should reference that kit purchase source and manufacturer part numbers, preventing the kit and its included components from being purchased twice. Initial modeling can use an assembled product envelope and declared interior space; component cut geometry must come from the matching manual, not a product photo.

## Minimum implementation to make these useful

1. The direct supplier channel covers Cedarbrook lining, foil, the prehung door and the freestanding benches. The foil uses the delivered 54 in width for a feasible cut layout. The quote length is derived from the rectangular blanks, including overlap and fold allowances. Confirm the actual supplier segments before cutting. The door option sets the exact rough opening and supports an outward swing check with entered hinge offsets.
2. Add cladding metadata for full stock width, effective cover and T&G profile. Repeating 3⅜ in solid boxes at a 3 in pitch creates false collisions; stock and installed geometry need separate treatment.
3. Apply bundle counts to linear-stock purchasing. The current linear BOM counts boards/bins; a six-board cladding bundle or four-board bench bundle needs `ceil(requiredBoards / packCount)`, without reusing leftover lengths as new purchased boards.
4. Model insulation as cuttable nonstructural batts/layers, with cavity depth and facing metadata. A soft batt is not a structural panel/support. Foil needs sheet/roll coverage and overlaps, not fake lumber thickness.
5. Add a preassembled door/kit part type with component provenance. Preserve leaf versus rough-opening/jamb dimensions and included hardware. Those assemblies should not inherit ordinary lumber cut operations.
6. Keep role evidence scoped: sauna lining, bench surface, framed-room insulation, foil, door or complete kit are different functions. A whole sauna kit does not grant every cedar board a sauna role, and a wall-lining qualification does not approve bench framing or heater clearance.

Meaningful acceptance examples: render raw Cedarbrook lining using its cover pitch with a bundle-aware purchase list; place the sourced door with its correct opening and outward motion; distinguish an R13 wall layer from ceiling depth; and place a Henley kit once with its true referenced interior volume. Each should retain outstanding heater/attachment/variant checks. No generic wood substitution should erase missing evidence.
