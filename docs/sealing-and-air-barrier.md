# Sealing wood joints and openings

A seam needs an assembly context and a purpose before Yardwork can suggest a detail. Keep siding drainage, air sealing, panel movement, adhesive bonding and fastener layout as separate checks.

| Assembly | Check to model | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sill plate on concrete or masonry | Record the continuous gasket at the bearing surface and any separate sill-to-rim or rim-to-subfloor air-seal runs. Confirm actual substrates, exposure and gaps. | [PNNL sill plates](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/air-sealing-sill-plates), [existing sill water management](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/water-managed-existing-sill-beams-and-sill-plates) |
| Bottom plate on subfloor | Record the intended air-barrier line, its interruptions and access before trim or finishes cover it. | [PNNL bottom plates](https://basc.pnnl.gov/diy-guides/air-seal-bottom-plates) |
| Rim joist and blocking | Identify which bays share the assembly boundary, measure each retained opening, and review the continuity and material of the blocking and its edge seal. Record fastener work separately. | [PNNL floors above unconditioned spaces](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/floor-above-unconditioned-basement-or-vented-crawlspace), [attached garage framing](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/garage-rimband-joist-adjoining-conditioned-space), [ENERGY STAR rim joists](https://www.energystar.gov/saveathome/seal_insulate/do-it-yourself-guide/sealing-air-leaks-basement) |

For a new sill on concrete or masonry, open **Sections → Plan sill gaskets**. Choose the actual horizontal wood part and measure the seated elevation and the continuous supporting length and width beneath it. Enter any center offset and the extra length needed for trimming and joint overlaps. The manufacturer calls for overlapping both end joints and corners and gives roll dimensions. The extra length stays a field measurement. Yardwork checks the entered bearing footprint, matches a full-width roll, and packs the measured strips without splitting an individual strip. The build guide lists the work before the sill’s step and shows what each roll leaves. Moving or cutting a sill calls for a fresh measurement. [PNNL sill plate detail](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/air-sealing-sill-plates) · [Owens Corning ComfortSeal technical sheet](https://images.thdstatic.com/catalog/pdfImages/dc/dc7a5b42-7c0c-4e72-9b9b-5e655782ecdc.pdf)

The reviewed 50 ft rolls span 3½, 5½ and 7½ inches. Use the listing title and published dimensions. The retailer’s shared description still refers to FoamSealR. Store quantities and prices use store snapshots. [Home Depot 1FS](https://www.homedepot.com/p/202084452) · [Home Depot 2FS](https://www.homedepot.com/p/202080947) · [Home Depot 3FS](https://www.homedepot.com/p/202274536)

For dry interior wood trim, open **List → Estimate trim sealant**. Measure the total seam length and the widest and deepest gap. Home Depot lists DAP Dynaflex 230 white model 18275, Internet #100035980, as one 10.1 fl oz cartridge. DAP publishes 55 linear ft at a 3/16 in bead. Gaps wider or deeper than that bead require measured trial coverage in Yardwork. Home Depot lists both 1 in and 2 in width limits, so Yardwork screens at 1 in wide and 1/2 in deep and sends larger openings to review for the delivered instructions, backing and geometry. Confirm the preview to record the purchase estimate and its measurements in the shopping list and build guide. Local price and quantity require a matching store snapshot. [Home Depot listing](https://www.homedepot.com/p/100035980), [DAP technical data sheet](https://images.dap.com/Dynaflex%20230%20White%20%26%20Colors_TDS_2.9.19.pdf)

An editable validation example can use short strips cut off the same trim stock to check the finish and bead before applying it on the actual assembly. Record those pieces as test offcuts, preserve their original cut source, and keep the measured seam lengths and any intentional gaps in the guide. Review whole-building ventilation and combustion equipment separately when the work affects the building enclosure.
