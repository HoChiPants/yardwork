# Sealing wood joints and openings

A seam needs an assembly context and a purpose before Yardwork can suggest a detail. Keep siding drainage, air sealing, panel movement, adhesive bonding and fastener layout as separate checks.

For a new sill on concrete or masonry, open **Sections → Plan sill gaskets**. Choose the actual horizontal wood part and measure the seated elevation and the continuous supporting length and width beneath it. Enter any center offset and the extra length needed for trimming and joint overlaps. The manufacturer calls for overlapping both end joints and corners and gives roll dimensions. The extra length stays a field measurement. Yardwork checks the entered bearing footprint, matches a full-width roll, and packs the measured strips without splitting an individual strip. The build guide lists the work before the sill’s step and shows what each roll leaves. Moving or cutting a sill calls for a fresh measurement. [PNNL sill plate detail](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/air-sealing-sill-plates) · [Owens Corning ComfortSeal technical sheet](https://images.thdstatic.com/catalog/pdfImages/dc/dc7a5b42-7c0c-4e72-9b9b-5e655782ecdc.pdf)

The reviewed 50 ft rolls span 3½, 5½ and 7½ inches. Use the listing title and published dimensions. The retailer’s shared description still refers to FoamSealR. Store quantities and prices use store snapshots. [Home Depot 1FS](https://www.homedepot.com/p/202084452) · [Home Depot 2FS](https://www.homedepot.com/p/202080947) · [Home Depot 3FS](https://www.homedepot.com/p/202274536)

To plan from the model, select two fitted or nearby wood parts in **Connections** and save a **Seal exposed seam** detail. Open **Plan trim sealant** and preview an edge. Measure keep-open ranges from the **Start** dot. The solid line shows the parts to seal, and the dashed line marks gaps left open. Record the real bead width and depth. The remaining runs appear in the guide and share the cartridge estimate in **List → Estimate trim sealant**. The guide points to a matching test scrap when the Cut pool contains an offcut from the same stock source. Verify access and keep the assembly's drainage passages clear. Use the field estimate for openings that interrupt the modeled faces. A cut or a change in relative fit requests a new measurement. Moving both members as a unit preserves the plan.

For a deeper dry interior trim joint, choose the measured fit of Frost King C21H (3/8 in) or C22H (1/2 in). Frost King calls for rod slightly wider than the gap and at least 1/4 in of space above the installed rod. In Yardwork, measure the widest opening and depth to the top of the fitted rod. Enter cartridge coverage from a trial. The cut plan fits whole runs across 20 ft rolls by diameter, lists matching leftovers and adds the reviewed roll sizes to the Home Depot handoff. Verify the fit at narrow points and the actual depth before applying sealant. [Frost King instructions](https://www.frostking.com/attachments/Product/136/documents/Instruction-Sheet-Caulk-Saver.pdf) · [Home Depot C21H](https://www.homedepot.com/p/100067266) · [Home Depot C22H](https://www.homedepot.com/p/100159362)

For dry interior wood trim, open **List → Estimate trim sealant**. Measure the total seam length and the widest and deepest gap. Home Depot lists DAP Dynaflex 230 white model 18275, Internet #100035980, as one 10.1 fl oz cartridge. DAP publishes 55 linear ft at a 3/16 in bead. Gaps wider or deeper than that bead require measured trial coverage in Yardwork. Home Depot lists both 1 in and 2 in width limits, so Yardwork screens at 1 in wide and 1/2 in deep and sends larger openings to review for the delivered instructions, backing and geometry. Confirm the preview to record the purchase estimate and its measurements in the shopping list and build guide. Local price and quantity require a matching store snapshot. [Home Depot listing](https://www.homedepot.com/p/100035980), [DAP technical data sheet](https://images.dap.com/Dynaflex%20230%20White%20%26%20Colors_TDS_2.9.19.pdf)

An editable validation example can use short strips cut off the same trim stock to check the finish and bead before applying it on the actual assembly. Record those pieces as test offcuts, preserve their original cut source, and keep the measured seam lengths and any intentional gaps in the guide. Review whole-building ventilation and combustion equipment separately when the work affects the building enclosure. [ENERGY STAR inspection guidance](https://www.energystar.gov/saveathome/seal_insulate/identify-problems-you-want-fix)

## Field cuts in pressure-treated lumber

**Guide → Review build preparation → Pressure-treated wood cuts** follows selected pressure-treated stock, recorded source faces, modeled holes and notches, and checked greenhouse foundation anchor bores. It includes cut scraps with recorded faces. Open a labeled part to inspect the saved geometry. For an existing shop scrap, confirm the original stock and any prior treatment. The guide asks you to check the delivered end tag, intended exposure, applicable AWPA M4 guidance and product label before covering the cut surfaces. Caulk, glue and finish coats have their own installation steps.
