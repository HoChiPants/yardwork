A seam needs an assembly context and a purpose before Yardwork can suggest a detail. Keep siding drainage, air sealing, panel movement, adhesive bonding and fastener layout as separate checks.

## Drywall at interior walls and ceilings

Choose **½ in drywall** in Panels and select the 8 or 12 ft sheet. Select **Cut part** to preview a straight score-and-snap cut at full thickness. Retain sound full-thickness scraps in the Cut pool. The buy list counts the exact source sheet, and the guide shows the cut and the utility knife requirement. Check actual factory tapers, supported seams, panel orientation, fastening and the air seal at the ceiling and wall boundaries. The panel is for dry, non-fire-rated interior assemblies. ## Rigid foam at floor rims

Use **Sections → Plan rim air sealing** or ask **Plan an idea** how to air-seal the rim joists. Pick a generated floor base and side. Choose the bays that lie on the enclosure boundary, measure each clear opening, and enter the distance from the rim top to the opening if it starts lower. Yardwork compares those measurements to the retained framing and checks the seated foam and edge space for modeled obstructions. The 3D outlines show the foam face after saving. Confirm backing at the top and bottom, access for the sealant, and any penetrations on site. [PNNL existing band joists](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/rigid-foam-board-insulation-existing-band-joists), [PNNL four-edge seal detail](https://basc.pnnl.gov/resource-guides/garage-rimband-joist-adjoining-conditioned-space)

Use the clearance and cutting loss measured with the foam and tool you intend to use. Dry-fit each insert first. Check the compatible sealant, substrate, actual moisture conditions, and how foam will be protected from fire and exposure. The written and printable guides have the exact rectangles, sheet cut order and reusable shapes. Matching stock and cutting-loss values share new 4 × 8 sheets across saved sides. Enter a labeled, measured 2 in offcut to use it before opening a sheet. Measure and label leftover foam after cutting before counting it toward another plan. A moved joist or a new obstruction puts the affected plan back into review and holds its planned sheet line in the Home Depot paste list. ### Measure the interior air seal

In the opening flashing planner, enable **Measure the interior air seal** after taking measurements at the interior edge of the installed unit. Record a width for each side, the smallest available cavity depth, and the planned seal depth. Measure again where the gap changes along a side. The preview moves the blue line to the center of the entered gap and keeps the exterior drain geometry visible. The check flags seals or backing that are too deep, and gaps outside the chosen material's fit.

- **Low-expansion foam** uses Great Stuff 99108862 with its included Smart Dispenser. The estimate scales the measured gap volume against the lower end of DuPont's reference of four to six 36 × 60 in windows per can at a 3/8 in wide, 1 in deep gap. Check coverage with a trial bead and follow the label for expansion, protection and ventilation. [DuPont foam specification](https://www.greatstuff.dupont.com/content/dam/dupont/amer/us/en/Greatstuff/public/documents/43-D100016-enNA.pdf) · [Single Home Depot can](https://www.homedepot.com/p/312450331)
- **Silicone over closed-cell rod** estimates the volume over the rod using DAP's published cartridge coverage. Yardwork suggests the smallest reviewed Frost King rod wider than each gap. Dry-fit for light compression, leave at least 1/4 in over the rod, and confirm substrate compatibility. The guide pools matching rod cuts with existing trim seams and shows leftover lengths. [DAP 08791 specification](https://images.dap.com/DAP%20Silicone%20Max%20All%20Purpose%20Silicone_TDS.pdf) · [Frost King installation](https://www.frostking.com/attachments/Product/136/documents/Instruction-Sheet-Caulk-Saver.pdf)
